# DERMDOC ad fix — image-generation brief for Codex

You generate corrected KEYFRAMES (still images) for short vertical skincare ads by the Indian brand DERMDOC
("Honest Science"). Each keyframe becomes the first frame of a 5-second AI video clip (Wan, 480p), so it must be a
clean, photographic, single moment — no collage, no text, no watermark, no UI.

## Output format (always)
- Vertical 9:16 (e.g. 1024x1536 or 1088x1920 portrait). Photorealistic, premium beauty-commercial lighting,
  shallow depth of field, real skin texture (pores, natural sheen) — not plastic, not airbrushed.
- NO text, letters, captions, logos or graphics anywhere in the image EXCEPT the printed product label itself.
- Save the final image exactly to the path given in the task.

## The products — must match the attached reference photos EXACTLY (shape, colours, cap, label layout)
### Purple Sunscreen (PS) — "DERMDOC Ultra Violet 4% Niacinamide with SPF 50 PA+++ Brightening Sunscreen", 60 g
- Soft white squeeze tube, flat crimped seal at the top end, label text: "DERMDOC" / blue "Honest Science" bar /
  "4% NIACINAMIDE WITH SPF 50 PA+++" / "BRIGHTENING SUNSCREEN"; a vertical lavender band with "ULTRA VIOLET" on the left.
- The cap is a WIDE, flat-topped, translucent lavender cap the full width of the tube end. The tube stands upright ON
  its cap. THERE IS NO NOZZLE, no pointed tip, no thin spout — never draw one. The cap is always on unless the shot is
  dispensing; when dispensing, cream comes out of a small round opening directly under the flip cap, flush with the end.
- Texture: smooth, light, pale LAVENDER cream (see ps_texture_drip.png). Not watery, not runny, not paste/mask/ubtan.
  When rubbed in it disappears completely: no purple left, no white cast, no residue — skin looks naturally radiant.
- Benefit language for visuals: INSTANT glow right after blending; purple tint cancels yellow-looking dullness.

### Kojic Night Cream (KNC) — "DERMDOC 2% Kojic Acid Night Cream, Glow Fusion Formula with Niacinamide & Alpha Arbutin"

  "Honest Science" bar / "2% KOJIC ACID" / "GLOW FUSION FORMULA with Niacinamide & Alpha Arbutin" / "NIGHT CREAM ALL SKIN
  TYPES" / round blue "NEW IMPROVED" badge / pale-yellow swatch rectangle.
- Texture: thick, rich, whipped WHITE cream with a soft peak (knc_tub_open.png). Not watery.
- It is a NIGHT cream: application only at night (warm lamp light). Results are seen next MORNING (soft daylight).

## The people
- Indian women, 24-32, DUSKY / medium-deep brown Indian skin tone (relatable, not fair). Keep the SAME skin tone
  before and after — products improve glow/evenness, they never lighten skin.
- No hair strands on skin in close-ups, no dandruff, no dark circles in "after" shots.
- "Before" (PS): dull, slightly yellowish/uneven-looking, matte, tired — but realistic, not dirty or diseased; never
  glowing. "After" (PS, instant): same face seconds later — even, smooth, dewy healthy glow, clearly visible difference.
- Dark spots (KNC "before"): REALISTIC hyperpigmentation — soft-edged brown sun spots / patches on cheekbones and
  upper cheeks, uneven tone, a few post-acne marks. NEVER round raised dots, never chicken-pox looking.
- "After" (KNC morning / after 2 weeks): visibly clearer, spotless-looking, smooth, glowing, healthy skin, same tone.

## Banned
Nozzles/spouts on the PS tube, missing cap, two products in frame (unless asked), wrong colours/label layout,
invented text, extra fingers, warped hands, mask-like thick application, residue, white cast, cartoon dark spots,
morning application of the night cream, fairness/whitening look.
